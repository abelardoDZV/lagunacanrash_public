How to get to Laguna Canrash

Laguna Canrash is in Taruscancha, in the district of San Marcos, Huari province, Áncash. The driving directions in this guide take you to the restaurant by the lake.

From Huaraz, Recuay and Conococha

From Huaraz, take the PE-3N toward Recuay and Cátac. At Conococha, turn onto the road toward Antamina and continue to Canrash. Allow about 3 hours by car from Huaraz. If you are starting in Recuay, join the route toward Cátac.

Huaraz route via Conococha Road information from the transport ministry

From Chavín de Huántar and San Marcos

After visiting the archaeological site or the National Museum of Chavín, you can drive onward to San Marcos. From there, follow the AN-111 to the restaurant. If Google Maps offers multiple routes, choose that road.

Route from San Marcos via the AN-111 Chavín archaeological site and museum

From Antamina or Huallanca

As a rough guide, the drive takes about 20 minutes from Antamina and an hour from Huallanca in Bolognesi province. Journey times depend on your starting point and traffic.

The restaurant by the lake

At the restaurant, we serve our own farm-raised trout daily from 7 a.m. to 7 p.m. Message us on WhatsApp about lodging or to check access before leaving.
